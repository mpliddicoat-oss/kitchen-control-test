▶ Kitchen Workflow

Kitchen Control follows the natural workflow of a professional kitchen.

1

Import Invoices

Scan supplier invoices to auto-create ingredient records and update prices.

2

Build Ingredients

Add allergens, pack sizes, supplier info and live costing data.

3

Add Allergens

Scan labels or manually assign allergens to stay compliant.

4

Create Yields

Track trim loss and calculate true usable ingredient costs.

5

Build Recipes

Combine ingredients and yields into fully costed recipes.

6

Create Dishes

Build customer-facing dishes with live GP% and allergen tracking.

7

Build Menus

Organise dishes into menus with automatic course ordering.

8

Print Allergens

Generate allergen matrices for front of house and compliance.

9

Generate Orders

Create ingredient order lists from your menus automatically.
