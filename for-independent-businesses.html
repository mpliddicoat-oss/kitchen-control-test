What's different about running it alone

Not a chain outlet following someone else's playbook — a kitchen where the person cooking is often the person doing the books, the ordering, and the allergen sheet, with nobody upstream checking any of it.

An independent business doesn't get a compliance team, a central purchasing system, or someone else's software licence. Whatever's in place, you put there yourself.

No procurement team cross-checking invoices. Whatever a supplier sends, you're the one who has to catch the mistake.
