How to know if it's time to switch

Three honest signs, not a sales pitch.

01

More than one person touches the numbers

The moment stock, prep or ordering needs two people looking at the same data on the same day, a shared spreadsheet starts fighting you instead of helping.

02

You've had a "which version is right" moment

If you've ever asked "is this the one from Tuesday or the one Dave edited," that's the tell. It's not a training problem, it's a tooling problem.

03

Onboarding someone new takes longer than a shift

A spreadsheet built by one person, for one person, takes explaining. Kitchen software built for kitchens shouldn't need a manual.

04

You're paying for it in time, not money

Spreadsheets are free to open and expensive to maintain. If you're the only one who can fix it when it breaks, that's a cost — it's just not on an invoice.
