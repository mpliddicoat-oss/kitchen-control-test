Kitchen Management Software UK: Compared

"Kitchen management software" covers a lot of different products doing very different jobs. Here's what's actually on offer in the UK right now, laid out honestly, so you can work out which category you actually need.

Kitchen Control · Guides

What "kitchen management software" actually means

It's not one thing. Under that banner you'll find allergen tracking, recipe costing, invoice scanning, stock control, POS and accounting integration, staff scheduling, HACCP compliance, nutrition data and customer-facing menus — and no product on the market does all of it. What varies between the real options is which of those jobs each one treats as core, and which it leaves out entirely.

The fastest way to waste money here is buying a platform built for a job you don't have — a multi-site stocktake and POS suite when you just need allergens and costing sorted for one kitchen, or the other way round.

What to actually check before you buy

Worth confirming for any option on this list
✓Which of the jobs above does it actually treat as core, and which are missing entirely — not "on the roadmap," missing?
✓Is it built and priced for a single site, or does the pricing only make sense once you're running several?
✓Can you see the price on the page, or is it a quote you only get after a demo call?
✓Can you bring your existing ingredient and recipe data in yourself, or does that depend on their onboarding team's schedule?
