What Kitchen Control does

Everything your kitchen needs.
Nothing it doesn't.

Built around how professional kitchens actually work — not how software companies think they do.

Allergen Management

Kitchen Control's allergen management software scans ingredient labels with your phone. Allergens are captured automatically and flow through every recipe, dish and menu. Full compliance with the Food Information Regulations 2014.

Live Recipe Costing

Every recipe shows live cost price and GP%. Scan a supplier invoice and ingredient prices update automatically across every recipe that uses them.

AI Invoice Scanning

Photograph a delivery invoice and Kitchen Control reads it — extracting every line item, updating costs and adding new suppliers automatically.

Recipe Import

Photograph a page from your recipe book — handwritten or printed — and Kitchen Control reads it. Ingredients are matched to your stock automatically, and anything it can't find is flagged clearly rather than guessed.

Yield Tracking

Record true yield after trimming and cooking. Your recipe costs reflect what actually goes on the plate, not what comes off the delivery truck.

Order List Generation

Build menus, set covers and Kitchen Control generates your order list automatically. No more working backwards from a menu at midnight.

Multi-User & Real-Time

Your whole team works from the same data in real time. Role-based permissions mean everyone sees exactly what they need to.
